This restores the noon yaw-first landing behavior and removes subsequent afternoon/evening landing, vertical-monitor, and vision endpoint changes. Logs and recovery archives remain outside the published source tree. Existing Git history is preserved by a new rollback commit.

The companion parameter file is a **manual archive**, with no automatic loading:

- 24 accelerometer, gyro and attitude-trim values restored from the full backup taken immediately before the evening six-face calibration.
- The pre-calibration learned hover thrust restored.
- `LAND_SPD_MS=0.5` and `PLND_RET_MAX=4` restored from the direct readback made before the afternoon change.
- Vehicle identity, automatic barometric references, storage startup state and flight/runtime counters are not overwritten.

All 27 values passed per-parameter independent reads, a final full-group read, and a separate complete 1015-parameter USB readback. After the physical powercycle, a new complete 1015-parameter readback verified that all 19 fixed restoration values persisted. The eight gyro bias/temperature values changed normally under the unchanged startup gyro-calibration policy. Firmware and sensor identity matched, the boot counter increased by one, and all other differences were limited to automatic pressure reference, SD startup adjustment and runtime/boot counters. The firmware image was not changed.

Two unrelated legacy extrinsic assertions also failed on the untouched noon candidate in the broader historical suite; that suite is not reported as fully passing. This is restoration evidence, not a new flight acceptance test.

Deployment status: completed on October 7 at approximately 00:29 (Asia/Shanghai). After the first attempt recovered its pre-attempt files, the retry restored 31 files and quarantined six later-added modules. All 37 target checks passed, the five services were active, and fresh telemetry confirmed disarmed, ON_GROUND, STABILIZE and IDLE. The camera produced advancing fresh frames. MAVROS kept the same process and invocation throughout the transaction. Local and device recovery archives were preserved.

Outstanding hardware finding: the post-reboot 10-second observation measured board supply at 3.965–4.271 V, and the flight controller reported `PreArm: Board (4.1v) out of range 4.3-5.8v`. This remains unresolved. Successful rollback does not clear the low-voltage condition or authorize another flight; this ground observation alone does not establish the cause of an earlier crash.
